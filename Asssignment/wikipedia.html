Kathmandu

Kathmandu is the capital and largest city of Nepal. It is located in the Kathmandu Valley in Bagmati Province. Kathmandu is known for its ancient temples, rich history, cultural heritage, and vibrant festivals. It is the political, educational, and economic center of Nepal.

Kathmandu is home to many UNESCO World Heritage Sites, including Kathmandu Durbar Square, Swayambhunath, Boudhanath, and Pashupatinath Temple. Every year thousands of tourists visit Kathmandu from different countries.



The city is famous for its traditional Newari culture, historical monuments, delicious foods, and religious harmony. Kathmandu is also one of the fastest-growing cities in Nepal.
